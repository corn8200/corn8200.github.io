Leave at 7:00, not 8:00. The drive is 3 hours 30 to 3 hours 45, not the 2.5 hours the map promised. Leaving at 7:00 puts you in Helvetia around 10:30 to 10:45 and keeps you behind the morning fog.

Read this part twice

There is no cell service in Helvetia. Not weak service. None. It drops for the last 17 miles of the drive and it does not come back until you leave the valley.

911 will not reach anyone by cell from the village. If something goes wrong, your options are the inn's wifi, the library, or satellite. All three are below.

Your phone will lie to you

Never follow anything labeled "WV 46." That is a different road on the other side of the state and it will take you about 90 miles the wrong way. You want County Route 46, which the signs call Adolph Road.

Refuse these if the map offers them. They are gravel, or worse, and some are barely roads:

  • Old Helvetia Road
  • Cassity Road
  • Pickens-Fairview Road
  • Anything called "Chemical & Helvetia Railroad"
  • Old Alexander Road

When the road signs and the phone disagree, the signs win. The village says so itself: GPS is not reliable there.

If you need help and have no bars

Your iPhone can reach emergency services by satellite with no cell signal at all. It works, but only if you do it right.

  1. Get outside. It will not work from inside the car or a building. You need open sky.
  2. Get clear of trees. Branches overhead will block it. Move to a field, a road, or a clearing.
  3. For an emergency, just dial 911. When the call cannot connect, the phone offers Emergency Text via Satellite. Tap Report Emergency and answer the short questions it asks.
  4. To reach John instead, open Messages and send normally. When it fails, the phone offers to send by satellite.
  5. Follow the arrow on the screen. Turn your body slowly until it lines up, then hold still. If it says you lost the satellite, turn slowly again until it reconnects.
  6. Be patient and stay put. One message can take 15 to 60 seconds. Do not walk around while it sends.

The nearest emergency room is Davis Medical Center in Elkins, about 75 minutes away. There is nothing closer.

Before you get there

  • Fill the tank before the mountains. There is no gas in Helvetia at all. The nearest pump is in Pickens, five miles south, and nobody could confirm it is open.
  • Bring cash. There is no ATM in the village.
  • Fog until about 9:00. Under a mile of visibility in the valleys early. Leaving at 7:00 means you are on the interstate through the worst of it and it has lifted by the time you reach the mountain roads.
  • The Hutte restaurant is closed Monday. John is buying groceries in Elkins and cooking at the suite, so do not count on finding food in town.
  • Last week's flooding was severe in these counties. If you come on water across the road, turn around. Do not guess at the depth. It is never worth it.

Driving home

You will be in two cars. Stay behind John and let him lead. For the first 40 minutes out of the valley neither of you will have a phone that works.

If you get separated, pull over in the next town with signal and wait there. Do not keep driving to find each other and do not try to call. Agree on that town before you pull out.
