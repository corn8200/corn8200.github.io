Fifteen Mythics, four bosses, and the sprite you asked about

Every Mythic in the season with exact steps, sorted by how much risk it costs you. Written for Zero Build duos with a small teammate, because that is how you play.

First, the sprite

There is no Jim Air Sprite and no Gem Air Sprite. Both readings come back empty. The Gem class is real and went live Thursday Aug 6, but it covers eight sprites and Air is not one of them. Air's only variants are Gold, Gummy, Galaxy and Holofoil.

The twist: Gem would have been a downgrade on Air. Gem's entire bonus is 30% less fall damage, and the plain Air Sprite already cancels fall damage completely. That redundancy is the likely reason Epic skipped it.

The Air Sprite itself is real, is Rare tier, and you can have one tonight. Section 05 has the full spec. If what you actually want is a Gem, section 07 has the one Gem you can get on purpose rather than by luck.

The two days that decide the rest of your season

Both of these are confirmed by Epic directly rather than by a guide site, and both land before the deadline. If you only play twice more, play these.

Saturday Aug 15: Unstable

Best day left

The season's closing Story Moment, at the Zero Point Stabilizer south of Frosted Flats. It happens inside ordinary Battle Royale and Zero Build matches, so there is no separate playlist to queue.

TimingStarts 2:00 PM ET. Be in a match by 1:55 PM ET. Damage at the Stabilizer is reduced from 1:30 PM ET and switched off entirely from 1:55, so nobody can third party you out of it. One time only, no replay announced.

Figures of 12:30 PM PT or 3:30 PM ET are circulating and are wrong. 2:00 PM ET is 11:00 AM PT.

Aug 15: Mythic Sprite Hours

Two windows

Immediately after Unstable, and this is the best shot you will get at a Mythic sprite. Two windows: 2:00 to 4:00 PM ET and 9:00 to 11:00 PM ET.

What changesEveryone starts with Seven Sliders and a Self-Revive. Mythic sprites appear more often, with an extra boost to base Grim and Zero Point. Loot quality is raised island wide, and extracting a sprite grants full healing plus the Slap effect.

Zero Point is widely rated the best sprite in the game. This window is the reason to care.

Monday Aug 10, then Aug 17

Mastery Monday

Starts 9:00 AM ET and runs a full 24 hours. Double Sprite Dust from every source, double Sprite XP, boosted Legendary and Mythic sprite spawns, and extra Portable Extractors around the map plus free ones from special quests.

Use it forLevelling one sprite to Mastery 5, which is the only sprite progress that persists into Season 4. Aug 17 is the last one.
01

Every Mythic, ranked by how little can go wrong

Guides that say "six Mythics" are quoting the June launch set. Counting the mobility items, event weapons and fishing, there are about fifteen live right now. Start at the top of this list, not with a boss.

Duck Race, two Mythics

Repeatable

The best value play in the season. No fight, and it resets every match, so you can attempt it every single game.

StepsGo to the large bridge directly north of Heatwave Harbor. Interact with the toy ducks to start the race. They swim downstream smashing boxes. Follow them to the finish line south of Heatwave Harbor, where cannons fire out the loot.
What dropsA chance at the Mythic Striker Pump Shotgun and the Mythic Chaos Exploder Rifle. Neither is guaranteed and both can roll Legendary. Even a failed roll pays out Chug Jugs, Gold Bars, ammo, materials and a Rift-To-Go, which is why it is worth running every match.

Third party risk: high. The cannons are loud and visible. Take the loot and leave, do not sweep the area.

Limitless Seven Sliders

Puzzle

Seven Sliders that never overheat and never lose durability. Unlimited mobility for the whole match, and the season's signature item. Under 90 seconds once you know the route.

StepsSinister Strip, the prison building in the northern section, opposite the giant sprite hologram. Downstairs into the basement tunnels. Find the locked cell. Deactivate the North Prison Security Grid behind its vent door, then the South one. Return to the cell, now unlocked, and take the Sliders. Rare Chests sit in the cell too.

Some guides call it the Electric Room. Same puzzle. An older single source claims an alternate at the Sunken Shores wishing fountain.

Spider-Man's Web-Shooters

Free

Mythic mobility, added Jul 30 and in the pool through season end. Web-swing across the map, chain swings, no fall damage while swinging. Works no matter which skin you wear.

Free routeInteract with a Spider-Man's Backpack webbed to a wall or cliff. Fourteen locations: Lifty Lodge, The Bus Stop, Latte Landing, Wonkeeland, Chopped Shop, The Battlewoods, Calamari Canyon, Frosted Flats, Fish Docks, Golden Grove, Shaken Sanctuary, Cluster Coast, Heatwave Harbor, Sinister Strip.
Guaranteed routeBuy from the Spider-Man NPC at The Battlewoods for 2,000 Gold Bars.

9mm Baba Yaga

Pet a dog

John Wick's Mythic pistol. The Legendary Lancehead Pistol is the fallback, so this is a chance rather than a certainty.

StepsTwo possible spawns: the southernmost building of Sinister Strip, or the underground sewers at The Battlewoods. The dog is caged and guarded. Clear the bodyguards, open the cage, pet the dog. It also completes a Battle Pass quest.

Sources disagree on whether the roll happens when you open the cage or when you pet the dog. Do both. Your kid will volunteer for this one.

Batman Grapnel Gun

2,000 gold

Long-range grapple with a 3 second cooldown that auto-deploys the Batman cape glider from height. Hooks are finite and cannot be refilled, so the item is discarded when spent.

StepsBuy from the Beach Bod Batman NPC for 2,000 Gold Bars, one per match. Also appears in Rare Chests. Available until Aug 19.

NPC location is reported inconsistently even within a single outlet. The island north of Golden Grove has two sources behind it. Hook count is either 26 or 27 depending on who you read.

Poison Ivy's Ranger Pistol

Duel

Rated the best of the three DC weapons. Increased fire rate while aiming down sights, low recoil.

WhereNorth of The Battlewoods, on the shoreline at the Bus Stop landmark. She does not appear on the map and never moves.
The trapYou must start the duel from the interaction menu before damaging her. Just killing her gives you nothing. Hiring her as a Specialist for 350 gold also gives you nothing.

Damage 36 | Headshot 72 | Mag 17 | Fire rate 6.1 | Reload 1.23s

Harley Quinn's Warforged AR

Duel

Mythic assault rifle, second best of the DC set by consensus ranking.

WhereSouthwest of Sinister Strip, along the shoreline. Same duel rule: start the duel first.

Duelling also gives a chance at a Batman Sprite, not a guaranteed one.

Catwoman's Rapid Fire SMG

Duel

Rapid fire, larger magazine, faster reload. Rated the weakest of the three DC Mythics, so do this one last.

WhereSouth shore of Wonkeeland. Start the duel first.

Mighty Impact Sniper Rifle

Vault

The most decisive item in the season on raw maths. 432 headshot damage with no damage falloff, so it one-shots a full health and shield player at any range in either mode, as long as you read the bullet drop.

StepsFarm Rare Chests until an Epic Vault Keycard drops. Take it to an Epic Sprite Vault: beneath The Shape at Sinister Strip, or east of Frosted Flats. Only two exist per match. The rifle is guaranteed once you are inside, which makes this the only non-random Mythic weapon in the game.

Damage 144 | Headshot 432 | Mag 1 | Fire rate 0.64 | Reload 2.55s

Mythic Goldfish

Fishing

A throwable that deals a flat 250 damage on a direct hit. Maximum health plus shield is 200, so a clean hit is an instant elimination. It is a projectile, not a consumable, so you pick it back up after every throw.

StepsFish. Prioritise golden fishing spots, most common in the rivers running toward the Zero Point at map centre. Rare Chests are the other source. A Pro Fishing Rod or a Harpoon Gun improves the roll.

Quoted at 0.04% per match by one source and roughly 1 in a million per cast by a datamine. Those numbers are irreconcilable, so treat this as a lottery ticket you buy while doing something else.

Bank Shot Pistol

Chests

The only weapon in the game that shoots around cover. One shot per magazine, reloads after every shot, and the round ricochets off walls, floors and ceilings with a trajectory preview while aiming. A bounced hit carries a 1.5x damage bonus. It cannot headshot, so the bounce is the weapon.

StepsChests, Rare Chests and fishing spots. The Mythic tier is reportedly most common in Vaults, particularly Renegade and Sprite Vaults.

Dark Voyager's Chaos Rifle

Boss

The easiest boss Mythic by a distance, and the one to do first. Added Jul 30, so it is missing from every older guide. Mythic version of the Chaos Exploder with a bigger magazine and faster reload.

Why it is easyVoyager Unleashed spawns at a random spot in the second circle, but unlike the other bosses he is permanently marked on the map as a cube icon. No hunting. He also has notably low health, and his drop is listed as guaranteed rather than a roll.
The trickHe fires cube projectiles and calls cubes down to crush you, so dodge the ground telegraphs. Wait for him to generate his cube shield, then dump damage. That is the window.

Reacher Extending Shotgun

Boss

Skeletor's drop, and the highest confirmed DPS of the three roaming boss weapons. Spread tightens the longer you fire, so it is unusually good at mid range for a shotgun.

Beating SkeletorRated possibly the easiest of the three roamers. He fires purple power blasts that knock you back. Fight at medium to long range with an AR and strafe the blasts.

DPS 176.4 | Damage 101 | Crit 145 | Mag 3 | Fire rate 1.75 | Reload 1.96s

The Voidblade's Burst Rifle

Boss

Mythic burst AR with increased accuracy while aiming down sights. The long-range answer of the boss set.

Beating the VoidbladeHighly mobile, uses verticality and dive-bomb attacks. Never stand still or she lands the overhead shot. Move laterally and use tracking weapons.

DPS 165.01 | Damage 29 | Crit 43.5 | Mag 33 | Fire rate 5.69 | Reload 2.38s

Wolfe's Maven Auto Shotgun

Boss

Full-auto shotgun that is more effective when you land fewer pellets, which makes it forgiving on sloppy hits and better in a real fight than its DPS number suggests.

Beating WolfeClose range, dashes constantly, demands tracking aim. Bring mobility and an SMG or shotgun, and keep moving.

DPS 146 | Damage 100 | Crit 150 | Mag 7 | Fire rate 1.46 | Reload 4.42s

Boss Mythics are a coin flip, not a reward. Killing Skeletor, Voidblade or Wolfe drops either the Mythic or the Legendary base version of the same gun. A widely shared datamine claimed a June hotfix made Mythics guaranteed, but guides updated well after that date still describe it as random, so plan on needing several kills for a specific gun. Voyager Unleashed is the exception, and it is also the easiest fight. Do that one.
02

How bosses actually work this season

This changed from previous seasons and most guides get it wrong. There are no boss POIs to memorise.

  1. Bosses do not exist at the start of the match. The three roamers only appear once the first storm circle finishes closing. Hunting early is wasted time.
  2. They share one pool of nine spawn zones and re-roll every match, so you cannot land somewhere to farm a specific boss.
  3. A red skull appears on your minimap within about 200 metres. Watch the minimap, not the full map. There is an audio cue too.
  4. Voyager Unleashed is different. He spawns in the second circle at a random spot but is permanently marked as a cube icon, visible to everyone from the moment he appears.
  5. Every boss drops 1,000 Gold Bars plus one healing item, either a Self-Revive, a Chug Jug or a Big Shield Potion. Even a Legendary roll pays for itself.

The nine spawn zones: among the trees north of Calamari Canyon; north of Heatwave Harbor; Collider Corridor north of Calamari Canyon; near the Extractor Point northeast of Calamari Canyon; east of the snowy stretch between The Battlewoods and Wonkeeland; the snowy area southwest of Latte Landing; west of The Battlewoods; near the trees where The Battlewoods meets the Zero Point; and the Extraction Site west of Golden Grove. They cluster around Calamari Canyon and Frosted Flats.

Doing this with a five year old. Treat a boss as a solo fight you happen to have a lookout for. Same rule as your gunfight plan: he gets behind cover and pings, you take the fight. Open from distance, make the boss walk to you, full shields before you start, then take the drop and leave immediately. The boss is not the danger. The team that heard the boss fight is.
Dying with a Mythic weapon costs you nothing beyond the match. Weapons have no cross-match persistence at all, and whoever kills you can pick yours up. There is zero reason to play passively to protect one. Use it aggressively. Sprites are the opposite, and confusing the two is the biggest strategic mistake available this season.

05

The Air Sprite, in full

Air Sprite

Rare tier

Increased sprint speed, increased sprint jump height, and complete fall damage negation. Jump height scales as the sprite levels.

The catch guides burySprinting and sprint-jumping still burn stamina. It makes you faster, not infinite.
Where it spawnsRegular Chests, Rare Chests, Sprite Chests, Sprite Locator Chests, and off players you eliminate. It favours high and mountainous terrain, so land high. Reported hotspots: Lifty Lodge, south of Latte Landing, Calamari Canyon.

Its four variants

Air has exactly four on top of the base: Gold, Gummy, Galaxy, Holofoil. No Gem, no Cube.

What they addGold gives triple sprite XP from eliminations, the only variant bonus that is solidly corroborated. Gummy adds Sprite Dust on extraction and Galaxy adds ammo on pickup, but published percentages for both contradict each other across sources. Holofoil is claimed to give a squad-wide 5% chance at rare variants from chests, single-sourced.

Better sprites than Air

If you want the strongest rather than the one you named, two beat it outright in Zero Build.

Zero Point SpriteSpawns a Shield Bubble Jr. whenever you heal yourself. It turns every heal into a protected heal, which is the highest-leverage effect in the game. Widely rated the best sprite there is.
Boss SpritePassively raises your max health and shield with no activation. Especially noticeable in Zero Build, where extra shield buys the seconds that win a close trade. Dropped by bosses.
Why no Gem Air Sprite exists. The eight live Gem sprites are Water, Earth, Duck, Demon, Zero Point, Aura, Grim and Llama. Gem Punk was revealed before release and then pulled, so it never shipped. Gem's class bonus is 30% reduced fall damage, and Air already negates fall damage entirely. One tracker site shows an Air row under Gem marked unreleased, which is a placeholder for a slot Epic never filled. The last content Thursday of the season is Aug 13, and no leak points at a Gem Air.
Throwing sprites is disabled. Epic switched it off after a duplication glitch, so any guide telling you to throw an Air Sprite for an updraft field is describing the old Chapter 6 version. You can only drop a sprite from your inventory now. Do not throw or drop one into an extraction crate either, because a bug made them register as the wrong type. Walk up and press Extract.
06

Keeping a sprite, which is the whole game

Surviving the match does not keep a sprite. Extracting keeps it. Everything else this season is downstream of that one rule.

  1. Keep it in your backpack, never in your hands. While you are holding a sprite you cannot use weapons at all.
  2. You cannot extract early. Extraction terminals stay locked until the first storm phase forms. There are 17 Extraction Sites, shown as white beams of light.
  3. Interact with the terminal to call the crate, wait for it to fall, then submit the sprite and wait for it to process.
  4. Calling a crate alerts nearby players. That cuts both ways: you can call one deliberately and back off to punish whoever comes.
  5. Die before extracting and the sprite is not destroyed, it drops for whoever killed you, keeping its level. That cuts both ways: taking sprites off players you eliminate is free value, and camping the walk-in to an extraction site is a real play.
  6. The crate sits for 1 minute 10 seconds, but collapses to 10 seconds the moment anyone puts a sprite in it. If a squadmate banks first you have ten seconds, which is brutal with a slow teammate.
  7. Winning the match banks whatever you are carrying. So if you are holding something rare in the last circles with a real shot at first place, hold it and play for the win instead of walking to a beam.
Two details worth the button presses: an Extraction Site Booster called at the panel gives a crate paying double Sprite Dust, and you trigger it by holding Y or Triangle before extracting. And if you interact with the terminal while not holding a sprite, it auto-extracts the first one in your quickbar reading left to right, which is exactly how people bank a junk sprite instead of the one they were protecting.
The item that fixes all of this: the Portable Extractor. 2,000 Sprite Dust, one per day. It banks a sprite on the spot, so you never have to walk to a beam or fight for a contested site. Buy one every single day for the rest of the season, because your Dust is about to be deleted anyway. Extraction pays 500 Dust for a Rare, 550 Epic, 750 Legendary, 650 Mythic.
07

Farming sprites on purpose

Two items make this dramatically faster than blind chest opening, and one Gem is obtainable on demand rather than by luck.

Lucky Locator

Gizmo

Guarantees a chest containing a sprite you have not yet collected. This is the single best collection-completion tool in the game and it comes from the Sprite Mastery track, Part 4.

Use them allGizmos are wiped at season end. Holding one on Aug 19 is a pure loss.

Quack Sprite

Multiplier

Every other sprite carried in your match inventory gains an extra 50% of your earned progress. If you are trying to fill a collection before the deadline, run this.

The one Gem you can get on demand

Guaranteed

Gem Duck is the only Gem sprite with a deterministic source. Everything else in the class is chest luck.

StepsFind the Suspicious Duck at Cluster Coast, which has four spawn points: the outdoor pool on the western side, the indoor pool in the adjacent building, the aquarium in that same building, and the golden duck fountain in the centre of the POI. It yields a rubber duck that opens the secret duck vault, no key or keycard needed, and the vault resets every match.

Vaults, all nine. Six small vaults open with an ordinary Vault Key and each holds a Rare Chest, Slurp barrels and a Sprite Chest: beneath the Mile High Retreat Supply Depot southeast of Lifty Lodge; southeast of The Battlewoods; southwest of Wonkeeland; southeast of Shaken Sanctuary; the gas station northeast of Sunken Shores; and beneath the Shady Eyes building east of Heatwave Harbor. Two large Sprite Vaults need an Epic Keycard: beneath The Shape at Sinister Strip, and east of Frosted Flats. The ninth is the Cluster Coast duck vault above. Keys drop randomly from chests, with a better keycard rate from Rare Chests.

Ignore every sprite drop-rate percentage, including any you see quoted elsewhere on this page. Epic has never published one. The numbers circulating trace to datamines at best, and a cluster of near-identical sprite tracker sites that appeared this season at worst. They contradict each other on the same items. The only variable you control is chest volume per match, so optimise that and skip the maths.

What dies on Aug 19

Two different things happen at the rollover and only one of them is fine.

WhatWhat happensDo this
Sprites you extractedSafe. They move into the Sprite Garden, a permanent Season 4 home for your collection.Nothing. Extract them and they are yours.
Sprites you never collectedRetired. Season 3 sprites stop appearing in Season 4. Epic says past generations may return but gives no date.Collect what you actually want this week.
Sprite DustWiped. Epic calls it seasonal housekeeping.Spend all of it. See the XP trick below.
GizmosAll wiped, including Portable Extractors and Lucky Locators.Burn every one.
Battle Pass tiersUnclaimed rewards are gone.Tap claim on everything, even what you do not want.
Every quest lineAll expire. None have expired yet.All 11 weeks are live right now. A backlog is a stockpile, not a loss.
The best trick in the season, and it takes two minutes a day. At any Extraction Site terminal you can trade 1,000 Sprite Dust for 20,000 XP, once per day. A Battle Pass level is roughly 80,000 XP. Doing that every remaining day is about 200,000 XP, or two and a half free levels, for almost no effort. One Rare extraction pays the 1,000 Dust. Since Dust is deleted on the 19th anyway, there is no reason not to.
If you want one thing to persist: get a single sprite to Mastery Level 5 and extract it. That permanently flags it as mastered and keeps it usable as a style on the Sprite Mastery Pod Back Bling into Season 4. Mastery Mondays give double sprite XP, and Aug 17 is the last one.
